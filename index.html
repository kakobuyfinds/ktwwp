From discovery to decision

How to use a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

A useful product list helps you identify exactly what you want and what remains uncertain. Keep the original listing beside your research so you can revisit the same item and variant.

Find and identify the item

Search a product type, then open its CAFANS listing. Record the source URL, seller reference if available, color and size. A product name alone may describe several different versions.

Compare fit and specifications

Check garment measurements or shoe sizing against something you already wear. Keep centimeters and inches separate. Ask about missing dimensions rather than assuming a familiar size label guarantees fit.

Confirm the ordering route

Review the agent options at the source. If you intend to use LoveGoBuy, confirm it accepts the original seller link and selected item. This page does not create an agent order or process payment.

Inspect before shipping

When warehouse inspection is available, check your actual order's quantity, color, selected size and visible condition. Resolve questions within the applicable return window before authorizing international shipment.

Useful answers

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

A LoveGoBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize potential purchases. It may be a traditional spreadsheet or a searchable web catalog. This independent guide presents a selection from CAFANS; it does not operate LoveGoBuy checkout.

Are these prices live or inclusive of shipping?

No. The displayed USD estimates were copied from CAFANS on November 19, 2026. Shipping, fees and other charges are separate, and current prices must be checked at the source. The selection is not automatically synchronized with inventory.

Does this page provide verified QC photos?

No. The catalog images are product listing photos from CAFANS. They are not presented as warehouse QC photos or proof of authenticity. If you obtain inspection photographs, confirm they relate to the exact seller listing, variant and order you are reviewing.

Where do the LoveGoBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons lead?

Both buttons open https://cafans.com. Product detail links open the corresponding CAFANS listing. Opening a link does not place an order, reserve stock or charge your account.

Can I order every listed item through LoveGoBuy?

Availability and agent acceptance are not verified by this page. Check the original listing and the current ordering options before committing to a purchase. Keep restricted-item questions, shipping eligibility and any missing information unresolved until the relevant provider confirms them.

Why can shipping change after an initial estimate?

An early estimate may use incomplete weight or parcel dimensions. Compare quotes using the same destination, packed parcel, service and included charges. Record whether an amount is provisional or a final payable quote.

Do saved products stay after I close the page?

No. The shortlist is held only in this page session and clears on reload. Save the product URLs in your own notes or spreadsheet if you want to keep them.
